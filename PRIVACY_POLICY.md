# Privacy Policy for TerraLink

**Effective Date:** August 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction
TerraLink ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile application designed for terrarium ecosystem and soil health monitoring. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect & How It Is Used
- **Bluetooth & Device Data**: TerraLink requests Bluetooth permissions (`BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`, `BLUETOOTH_SCAN`, and Bluetooth usage on iOS) exclusively to discover, pair with, and receive sensor data from your physical microcontroller hardware (e.g., HC-05/HC-06 modules). We do not track or transmit Bluetooth identifiers to external servers.
- **Location Services**: Android requires location permission to perform Bluetooth LE / SPP scanning. Location data is processed locally on your device for scanning purposes only and is never recorded, transmitted, or sold.
- **Sensor Telemetry**: Environmental readings (temperature, humidity, moisture, NPK, pH, EC) are processed locally on your device for real-time dashboard display and local analytical history.
- **User Authentication**: If you choose to log in using Google Sign-In or Email authentication, your account credentials are managed securely via OAuth / authentication providers. Guest mode requires no personal authentication information.

## 3. Data Storage & Security
All telemetry data, automation rules, and local settings are stored locally on your mobile device. We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data or device diagnostics to third parties.

## 4. Third-Party Services
TerraLink may use standard platform services (such as Google Sign-In SDKs) which collect minimal usage data strictly required for session management in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our GitHub repository: [https://github.com/Astraa14/terralink](https://github.com/Astraa14/terralink).
